OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy for TS DA Arrears Calculator

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the TS DA Arrears Calculator ("we", "our", or "the application") handles user data. The application is developed as a public utility tool for Telangana State Government employees to calculate Dearness Allowance (DA) arrears and monthly installment split (CPS & GPF).

1. Information Collection and Storage

We respect your privacy. This application does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • All salary, Basic Pay, and increment inputs are processed strictly on your device (client-side).
  • We do not collect names, employee IDs, PRAN numbers, bank account numbers, or GPS location data.
  • No data is transmitted to any external server or third-party database.

2. Offline Storage & Cache

The application uses standard browser Service Worker cache technology to allow offline access so that calculations can be performed without an active internet connection. This cache contains only static assets (HTML, CSS, JS, and app icons) and does not store user personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

4. Children's Privacy

The application is designed for adult government employees and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
